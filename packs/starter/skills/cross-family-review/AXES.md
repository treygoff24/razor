# Axis library: by package type

A starting crew per package type: the **fidelity helper** does what an auditor does (accuracy against sources; give it the authoritative reference paths) and the **cold-read helper** does what the real first-time reader does under pressure (tell it who that reader actually is). Add a third discipline when the package's verbs call for one: a short seller for a thesis that rests on one claim, a forensic accountant for anything with figures, hostile counsel for anything that will be quoted back. Adapt wording freely; keep each helper to 3 to 5 axes, since more dilutes attention.

## Annotated legal or statute package

(annotations, section-by-section guides, bill pages)

Fidelity helper:
1. Annotation-vs-text fidelity: does each annotation claim match what the quoted statutory text actually does? Hunt for asserted mechanisms, thresholds, deadlines or actors the text does not contain, and numbers that differ (days, percentages, multiples, "plus costs and interest" clauses).
2. Tripwire audit (the frozen-claims list, verbatim).
3. Cross-file consistency: overview and summary claims against the per-section files; status fields against the changelog; version-stamp facts against the draft date.
4. Superseded-content leakage: prior-version mechanics described as current.

Cold-read helper (reader: the principal defending this text under oath, on a phone):
1. Self-containedness: insider shorthand, unglossed acronyms, facts the page never establishes.
2. Register: law-review or press-release voice where plain English belongs.
3. Defensibility: absolutes ("nothing to fear"), legal-certainty claims on contested law, any line a hostile staffer could quote back.
4. Glanceability: blocks too dense to scan under pressure.

## Policy memo or paper

Fidelity helper: every number, date, name and citation against the stated sources; internal consistency of figures across sections; tripwire audit; whether the recommendation follows from the evidence presented or from evidence not in the document.

Cold-read helper (reader: the named audience, such as a staffer, a principal or an agency official): audience fit (gloss debt, assumed context); the strongest counterargument the memo fails to preempt; concession opportunities that would buy credibility; directive-voice or reader-management tells.

## Outreach batch

Fidelity helper: recipient facts against the contact records (names, titles, offices, thread history); no stale contacts; calendar and gift-rule tripwires; each draft's ask matches the tracker.

Cold-read helper (reader: the busy recipient, eight seconds): would they know what is asked and by when; anything presumptuous, over-warranted or flattering; whether it sounds like the sender.

## Finance model or quantitative package

Fidelity helper: formula spot-audit on the highest-stakes outputs; input assumptions against their cited sources; unit and magnitude sanity; tripwire audit on headline figures.

Cold-read helper (reader: a skeptical analyst): which single assumption, if wrong, breaks the conclusion; whether it is defended; whether the summary-tab claims match the underlying tabs.

## Generic document package

Fidelity helper: claims against supplied references; internal contradictions across files; tripwire audit.

Cold-read helper: name the real reader first, then: self-containedness, register, defensibility, structure.
